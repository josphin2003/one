What Is Graphic Design?

The craft of communicating ideas visually — combining images, typography, color, and layout to inform, persuade, or engage an audience.

RASTER vs. VECTOR
PHASE 01 01
RASTER

Learn how raster images are created using pixels and understand their characteristics, quality, and resolution.

  • Made of pixels; resolution-dependent
  • Best for photographs & rich textures
  • Loses quality when scaled up
  • File types: PSD, JPG, PNG, TIFF
PHASE 02 02
VECTOR

Understand the website structure, define section goals, and identify the required content and assets.

  • Made of math paths; resolution-independent
  • Best for logos, icons & illustrations
  • Best for logos, icons & illustrations
  • File types: AI, EPS, SVG, PDF

Typefaces & Pairing

Aa

Used for printed materials.

DISPLAY FONTS

Bold, decorative fonts used sparingly for headlines, posters & logos — designed
to grab attention, not for body text.

FONT PAIRING
  • Pair a serif header with a sans-serif body (or vice versa)
  • Limit a design to 2–3 typefaces max
  • Use weight & size to create contrast, not more fonts
